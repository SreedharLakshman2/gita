Privacy Policy — Gita

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Gita is a Bhagavad Gita reading app. Reading language, bookmarks, and progress stay on your device. There is no account and no cloud library in this version.

Information stored on your device

  • Selected language, text size, and appearance
  • Bookmarks, favorites, and continue-reading position
  • A local reading streak and verse history

This data is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

Audio

Recitation uses a Krishna-like male voice chosen from the speech voices already on your device (Web Speech on the web, AVSpeech on iOS). Audio is not uploaded. You can stop playback at any time.

Notifications

If you turn on verse reminders, a local notification is scheduled on this device for a morning reading. The reminder is not sent through our servers.

Information we do not collect

  • No login or profile name
  • No location tracking for our servers
  • We do not sell reading history

Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Clear site data or delete the app to remove local reading data.
